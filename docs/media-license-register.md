# Media license register

Every demonstration asset shipped with Workout Conductor is listed here with its source and
license before it is committed. The manifest in `src/catalog/media/mediaManifest.ts` and the data
in `src/catalog/media/exerciseMedia.ts` carry the same source, licence and credit at runtime.
Assets that are not in this register do not ship.

## Rules

- Only original media created for this project, media whose licence permits redistribution in a
  public GitHub repository and GitHub Pages site (CC BY, CC BY-SA), or public-domain media with
  verified terms. Each file is kept with the app; nothing is hotlinked.
- No downloads from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, stock sites whose terms forbid redistributing the
  file on its own (Pexels, Pixabay), exercise websites or commercial apps, and nothing taken only
  because it is publicly viewable. No logos or channel marks are kept: a clip that carried one is
  cropped clear of it.
- Each demonstration shows the exercise's own variation: the equipment, the position and the
  path. Where a detail differs (a kettlebell for a dumbbell), the demonstration says so under it,
  and where the How to text named a different setup, the text was changed to match (see
  `docs/exercise-howto-sources.md`).
- Credit: the demonstration names its source and licence under it, with the title, author,
  links and changes under "About this video"; Settings, About lists every credit. Each
  adaptation of a CC BY-SA original is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- U.S. military footage (DVIDS) shows the notice DVIDS asks every user to show: "The appearance of
  U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement."
- User-owned custom media (`CustomMediaSchema`) is stored only in the user's browser and backups;
  it is never committed and is kept separate from the licensed set.
- Every production-enabled exercise has a working still and demonstration; the tests enforce
  that each resolves to a registered asset whose files exist and that every credit is complete.

## Licensed demonstrations (Maintenance 25, item 7)

72 of the 83 exercises: 57 filmed clips and 15 loops of drawings. Each clip is cut to a short loop
of the movement without sound, its last eight frames (a third of a second) blended into its first so
that its last frame is the one just before its first and it plays on without a jump, and resized to
at most 480 pixels wide and 512 high (H.264 MP4, no audio); 45 are cropped to the lifter; 3 show one
camera's whole panel of a DVIDS split screen (overhead-press, dumbbell-row, farmer-carry);
machine-chest-press is cropped only of its film's black bars; and 8 keep the whole frame
(barbell-bench-press, incline-barbell-bench-press, dumbbell-bench-press, incline-dumbbell-press,
dip, rear-delt-fly, barbell-row, standing-calf-raise). Its first frame is the still (WebP). Each
drawing loop shows Everkinetic's start and end drawings in turn with a short blend. Where a
demonstration differs from the steps in a detail, a note under it says so, and the steps hold (ten
notes, listed in `src/catalog/media/exerciseMedia.ts`). Files are named by their content,
`public/media/exercises/<exercise>.<hash>.mp4` and `.webp`, so a corrected clip reaches a phone that
kept the old one. In all, 5.4 MB (millions of bytes): the stills (0.75 MB) are kept for offline use
at install, and each clip (4.6 MB together) is kept the first time it plays.

while it resolves to a diagram.

## Your own demonstration (user media)

- Open an exercise's Options (or its page in the Library), then tap the demonstration (or the
  "Your GIF" button) to pick a GIF, photo, or short video from the phone, up to 3 MB. It replaces
  the catalog's demonstration on the card, in How to and in the details, with Replace and Remove
  under it.
- User media is stored inline in the on-device database (`customMedia`, one per exercise),
  travels with a full backup, and is never uploaded anywhere or committed to this repository.
- On the workout, the card of the exercise under way loops its demonstration for as long as that
  exercise is in front; its clip (or the user's own video) rests where it is out of view, in the
  background or under a sheet, and plays on once back; the other cards show the still. The workout's How to's Pause holds it on
  its still, with a pause mark (WCAG 2.2.2), until Play there. Phones set to reduce motion get the
  still, with Play in How to.
- A licensed clip can be paused and slowed to half speed in How to; the user's own video or
  moving GIF, and a diagram's loop, have Pause and Play there too, and start paused under reduced
  motion; on the card the user's own GIF rests on its first frame while paused in How to, and a
  GIF, WebP or PNG of their own loops for good whatever its file says.
