# Training dose, frequency and minutes: what a week of limited time can deliver

_Research report, 2026-10-11, for the week-model and time-intelligence proposal. Carried into the repository on 2026-10-17 for the cloud handoff (docs/handoff/CLOUD_HANDOFF.md). EB = evidence-based, PJ = product judgement._

The raw notes and saved abstracts stayed on the working machine and are not in the repository; every claim below cites its PubMed ID (or DOI) so it can be checked again.

63 PMIDs were verified. The Remmert 2025 preprint (no PMID) is flagged. The retracted Barbalho trials are excluded.

## Bottom line
- **Growth** rises with weekly hard sets, with diminishing returns.
  - About 4 sets per muscle a week already grows muscle (Iversen 2021 34125411; Schoenfeld 2017 27433992: <5 / 5-9 / 10+ sets gave about 5 / 7 / 10%).
  - 10-20 is the near-full range (ACSM 2026 41843416; Baz-Valle 2022 35291645).
  - Above 20 the gain is small or inconsistent: trained-lifter trials conflict (Brigatto 2022 31868813; Aube 2022 32058362; Moreno 2025 39557664; Barsuhn 2025 39665246). Individualised volume, 1.2x the lifter's usual, beat a fixed 22 sets (Scarpelli 2022 32108724).
  - Indirect sets count 0.5 (Pelland 2026 41343037).
- **Strength** levels off early.
  - 2-3 hard sets per main lift per session at >=80% 1RM, >=2 a week (ACSM 2026).
  - Floor: 1 set at about 70-85%, 1-3 times a week (Androulakis-Korakakis 2020 31797219).
  - Three 13-minute sessions a week matched 40- and 68-minute sessions for 1RM in trained men (Schoenfeld 2019 30153194).
- **Frequency and session length** barely matter at equal weekly sets.
  - Schoenfeld 2019 30558493 supersedes the 2016 "twice a week"; Ramos-Campo 2024 38595233: splits equal full-body.
  - Once a week progresses older adults (Taaffe 1999 10522954; Nuzzo 2024 38509414) and maintains gains (Ronnestad 2011 21873897).
  - Per-session gains plateau at about 11 fractional sets per muscle (preprint), so split a muscle across >=2 sessions above about 10 sets a week (judgement).
  - Sessions of 15 min x9 equal 45 min x3 (Kilen 2015 26506198); 15 x8 equal 60 x2 (Kilen 2020 32371120); 7, 20 and 60 min equal in Dalager 2015 25816746.
- **Minutes per set including rest:**
  - About 1.9 at 90 s (Schoenfeld 2019 full text: 7/21/35 sets took 13/40/68 min).
  - 24 sets: 58 min straight versus 35 min as supersets (Andersen 2022 35783742).
  - Rest: >60 s helps a little, nothing detectable >90 s (Singer 2024 39205815).
  - Trained strength needs >2 min rest (Grgic 2018 28933024).
  - Supersets about 37% shorter (Zhang 2025 39903375; only 3 long-term trials; a cost to pulling strength for compound pairs, Iversen 2024 39072654).
  - Drop sets take 1/2 to 1/3 of the time (Sodal 2023 37523092); rest-pause equal (Tsartsapakis 2026 41718208); no overall advantage for advanced methods (Cowley 2026 41951916).
- **Maintenance:**
  - Young adults: 1 session a week, 1 set per exercise, load kept, held 32 weeks. Older adults lost size even on a third of the dose (Bickel 2011 21131862).
  - Older adults: 2 sessions of 2-3 sets (Spiering 2021 33629972).
  - Half the volume, 1-2 times a week, held 8 weeks (Tavares 2017 28316261).
- **Detraining:**
  - Strength holds about 3 weeks (McMaster 2013 23529287).
  - 2 weeks off cost nothing (Hwang 2017 28328712); 1 week off, nothing (Coleman 2024 38274324).
  - Breaks are regained (Ogasawara 2013 23053130; Halonen 2024 39364857).
  - Losses are larger over 65 (Bosquet 2013 23347054; Grgic 2022 36360927).
- **Health:** WHO, >=2 days a week of all major muscles (Bull 2020 33239350). Mortality is lowest at about 30-60 minutes a week, J-shaped (Momma 2022 35228201; Shailendra 2022 35599175). Minimum 1-2 a week alongside aerobic activity (Coleman CJ 2022 35953241).
- **Older adults:**
  - Low volume suffices for function and size; strength wants more (Radaelli 2025 39405023).
  - ICFSR: 2-3 a week, building to 2-3 sets (Izquierdo 2021 34409961).
  - >2 sessions add little (Kneffel 2021 32948100); frail adults need 2, best 3 (Nagata 2023 37774931).
- **Effort:** 1-2 RIR grows muscle like failure does (Refalo 2024 38393985). Growth improves nearer failure; strength does not (Robinson 2024 38970765). A hard set means <=3 RIR.

## Recommended numbers (E = evidence, P = product judgement; store as sourced data, not code constants)
- **Hypertrophy sets per muscle per week:**
  - Floor 4 (E).
  - Beginners start 6-10 (P).
  - Trained 10-20 (E); 20-30 only as an opt-in (P).
  - Older adults 4-10 over 2-3 sessions (E/P).
- **Strength:** 2-3 hard sets of >=80% per main lift per session, >=2 a week. Floor: 1 set at 70-85%, twice a week (E).
- **Maintenance:** young adults 1 session a week, 1 set per exercise, load kept; older adults 2 sessions of 2-3 sets (E).
- **Health:** 2 days a week of all major muscles, about 30-60 minutes in total (E).
- **Indirect credit:** 0.5 for growth (E). Strength: 1.0 for the same pattern, <=0.5 otherwise (P).
- **Frequency:** default 2 per muscle; 1 allowed for maintenance, health or the time-short (E). Split above about 10 sets a week (P).
- **Minutes per set** (incl. rest):

  | Set type | Minutes | Basis |
  |---|---|---|
  | Compound, for growth | 2.0-2.5 | E |
  | Isolation | 1.5-2.0 | P |
  | Heavy strength | 3.0-3.5 | P, from Grgic |
  | Superset or drop set | 1.3-1.5 | E |

  Add 3-5 min of warm-up per session (P). Refine from the user's logged times.
- **Weekly whole-body minutes** (P model, anchored to Schoenfeld 2019; about +/-30%):

  | Goal | Maintain | Slow progress | Near-full |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Health | - | 30 | 60 |
  | Strength | 15-25 | 30-45 | 60-90 |
  | Hypertrophy | 15-30 (older 30-45) | 30-100 | 120-250 |

- **Persistence:** never flag one short week (E). Use a rolling 4-week average; show "too little time" after >=3 consecutive weeks below the goal's floor (E from McMaster; P for the window). A planned week below the floor is flagged at once (P).
