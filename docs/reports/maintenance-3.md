# Maintenance 3: smarter alternatives, and learning from overrides

Third round of the owner's list: alternatives ranked by what a swap costs, with a reason per
candidate, and engines that follow the lifter's own habits instead of repeating themselves.

## Delivered

- **Smarter alternatives.** The ranking now reads your history with each candidate (when, and
  at what load and reps), how loaded each muscle already is this week, the joints you flagged
  today, and whether the coach route for the current lift is asking for a variation. Each
  candidate in the details sheet shows up to two specific reasons and the strongest reason
  against, instead of one generic line. High stress on a joint that hurts today excludes a
  candidate outright.
- **Learning from load overrides.** When you logged the first working set above the suggested
  load in three of the last four sessions, the next target steps up one more step and says why;
  three of four below, it steps down. Deloads, resets, returns from a break, and estimates are
  never biased.
- **Learning from declined offers.** The coach card gains one small "Not now" link beside its
  action. A declined offer is remembered by source and lift, stays away for seven days, and after
  two declines stays away for good. Safety signals are never put away. Declines live in the meta
  store and are backed up with everything else.
- **Surfaces unchanged** apart from the one text link. Nothing is applied without a tap.

## Verification

- Unit: signals building, ranking with history, load, pain, and route reasons, override bias and
  its effect on the next target, decline recording and suppression, the card's Not now, and
  decline persistence across a reload.
- Browser: the alternatives sheet shows the reasons line; Not now puts a stalled-lift offer away
  and the card stays quiet after a reload.

## How to check on the phone

1. Open any exercise's details from Today and read the alternatives: each has reasons such as
   "last done Sep 6: 60 lb × 8" or "adds triceps, behind its weekly target".
2. On a coach card with an action, tap "Not now": the card goes quiet and the offer stays away.
3. Lift above the suggested load on a lift for three sessions in a row: the next "Why this
   target" says the target stepped up to meet you.
